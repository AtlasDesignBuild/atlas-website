Where we work

Towns we serve in the Lehigh Valley

Easton

From College Hill Victorians to rowhomes and twins in the West Ward and South Side, much of Easton's housing dates to the late 1800s and early 1900s. Our Easton work often starts with what is behind the walls: older plumbing, plaster and framing that needs to be brought up to date before new finishes go in.

Forks Township

Most Forks Township homes were built from the 1980s onward, along with a number of older farmhouses. Projects here usually focus on layout, storage and finishes rather than structural correction, with farmhouses as the exception.

Palmer Township

Palmer Township combines mid-century ranches and split-levels near Easton with newer subdivisions. Mid-century homes often benefit from opening the main floor, while newer homes are ready for custom finishes.

Williams Township

South of Easton, Williams Township is largely rural, with farmhouses that have grown over generations and custom homes on larger lots. Projects here often involve well water, septic systems and joining old construction with new.

Bethlehem Township

Between Bethlehem and Easton, Bethlehem Township spans postwar homes, 1960s and 1970s split-levels and newer colonials. Projects here often focus on opening split-level layouts and finishing builder-grade homes.

We also work in other nearby Lehigh Valley towns. Call us to confirm your address is within our service area.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which Lehigh Valley towns do you work in?

We work regularly in Easton, Forks Township, Palmer Township, Williams Township and Bethlehem Township, and in other nearby towns. If you are not sure whether your home is within our service area, call us and we will tell you.

Do you handle permits in the City of Easton and the surrounding townships?

Yes. The City of Easton and each township run their own permit process, and exterior work in Easton's historic districts can require additional review. We prepare the drawings, file the applications and schedule inspections.

Do you work on older homes?

Yes. Much of our Lehigh Valley work is in homes built before 1950. We assess plumbing, electrical, framing and moisture before design is finalized so the budget reflects the house as it is.

Do you still work in Bucks County and Montgomery County?

Yes. We continue to work across Bucks County and Montgomery County alongside our projects in the Lehigh Valley.
