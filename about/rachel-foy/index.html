About Atlas Design & Build · Co-founder

Rachel Foy

Rachel Foy is a co-founder of Atlas Design & Build, the husband and wife owned design build firm she runs with her husband, John J. Foy. She is certified in interior design and came to the work from a background in digital design.

Role and background

What Rachel leads at Atlas Design & Build

Rachel leads design development, client communication and the client experience at Atlas Design & Build. She works alongside John on every project, shaping the design with each client and keeping them informed at every stage, from the first conversation through the final walkthrough.

Rachel also writes our remodeling guides, which cover costs, timelines and design decisions for homeowners across Bucks County, Montgomery County and the Lehigh Valley.
