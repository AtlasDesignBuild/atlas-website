Tile installation in Easton, PA

Easton is one of the most distinctive communities in the Lehigh Valley. College Hill, home to Lafayette College, holds one of the region's largest collections of Victorian and Georgian Revival homes, most built between 1830 and 1940, while downtown Easton carries its own mix of historic rowhomes and a genuine ongoing revitalization. At Atlas Design & Build, we work with homeowners across Easton on custom tile installation for bathrooms, kitchens and floors, as a standalone project or as part of a larger remodel. We live in Easton and manage every project directly.

A tile project does not have to be a full renovation

Not every tile job needs a demolition crew or a new floor plan. If your bathroom needs a new shower surround, your kitchen needs a backsplash or your entryway needs a floor that holds up to daily traffic, we take that on as a standalone project. We handle the substrate prep, waterproofing and layout with the same care we bring to a full remodel, scaled to the size of the job.

Older construction

Remodeling older homes in Easton

A significant share of Easton's housing stock predates modern wiring and plumbing standards. College Hill's Residential Historic District holds some of the largest homes in the city, most built between 1830 and 1940, and downtown Easton has its own historic district as well. Knob and tube electrical, galvanized plumbing, lath and plaster walls and settling foundations are common in homes of this age. These are not obstacles. They are part of what makes Easton's older homes worth renovating carefully. We are EPA Lead Safe certified, which is required for any home built before 1978. We plan for the realities of older construction rather than being surprised by them mid-project.

Homes within College Hill's historic district may carry additional review requirements for exterior changes. For tile projects this rarely affects the interior work, but if your project touches windows, exterior openings or additions, we factor that review process into the timeline from the start.
