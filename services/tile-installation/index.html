Tile installation

Tile installation in Bucks County, PA

Tile is one of the most common requests we hear from homeowners, and it does not have to come attached to a full kitchen or bathroom remodel. At Atlas Design & Build, we work as a tile contractor on standalone projects, from a single shower surround to a full home's worth of floors, not only as part of a larger renovation. We bring the same standard of planning, waterproofing and finish work to a standalone tile job as we do to any full renovation. We work throughout Bucks County and Montgomery County, including Doylestown, Newtown, Yardley, Langhorne, Warrington and the surrounding communities.

A tile project does not have to be a full renovation

Not every tile job needs a demolition crew or a new floor plan. If your bathroom needs a new shower surround, your kitchen needs a backsplash or your entryway needs a floor that holds up to daily traffic, we take that on as a standalone project. We handle the substrate prep, waterproofing and layout with the same care we bring to a full remodel, scaled to the size of the job.

Our scope

What our tile installation services include

Every tile project starts with a conversation about the space, the traffic it sees and the look you are after. From there we handle layout, substrate prep, waterproofing and installation, start to finish.

Full height showers, tub surrounds, niches and floor tile, waterproofed and installed to hold up under daily use.

Kitchen backsplash

Custom pattern layouts, mosaic and natural stone backsplashes installed cleanly around cabinets, outlets and appliances.

Kitchen and bathroom floor tile

Porcelain, ceramic and natural stone floors set level and true, with attention to transitions and grout lines.

Fireplace surrounds and hearths

Tile and stone surrounds that reset the focal point of a living room or family room.

Mudroom, entry and laundry tile

Durable floor tile for the rooms that take the most daily wear.

Natural stone and mosaic work

Marble, travertine and mosaic detail work for accent walls, floors and shower niches.

Outdoor patio and walkway tile

Frost rated tile and stone set for Bucks County's freeze-thaw climate.

Tile repair, regrouting and replacement

Cracked tile, failing grout and dated patterns replaced without redoing the whole room.

Our approach

Why Bucks County homeowners choose Atlas for tile

Tile work fails when the prep underneath it is rushed. Waterproofing membranes, proper substrate and correct layout planning determine whether a shower holds up for twenty years or needs to be redone in five. We treat a standalone tile project with the same discipline as a full remodel because that is what makes the finished work last.

We are an owner-operated team based in Bucks County. One of us is on site managing the work directly, not handing it off to a rotating crew. That is true whether the project is a full kitchen or a single bathroom floor.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you take on tile projects that are not part of a larger remodel?

Yes. We work as a tile contractor on standalone projects, from a single shower surround to a full floor. You do not need to be planning a full kitchen or bathroom remodel to work with us on tile.

How much does tile installation cost in Bucks County?

Cost depends on the size of the space, the material and the condition of what is underneath the existing tile or flooring. We walk the space and give you a detailed written proposal before any work begins, so you know the full scope and price before committing.

How long does a tile project take?

A single shower surround or backsplash typically takes one to two weeks once materials are on site. Larger floor projects or multi room jobs run longer. We build the schedule with you upfront and share it in writing.

Do you handle waterproofing?

Yes. Every wet area we tile, showers, tub surrounds and bathroom floors, gets a proper waterproofing membrane before any tile goes down. This is not an upgrade, it is part of how we build every wet space.
