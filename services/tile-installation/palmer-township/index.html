Our scope

What our tile installation services include in Palmer Township

Tile work in Palmer Township homes covers a few recurring projects.

Removing mortar-bed tile

Original tile in mid-century bathrooms is often set in a mortar bed more than an inch thick. We remove it down to the framing and rebuild the walls flat and plumb, which gives new tile a sound base.

Tiled showers with full waterproofing

Whether we are replacing a fiberglass unit or a builder-grade tiled shower, every shower gets a continuous waterproofing membrane, a properly sloped pan and sealed niches and benches.

Kitchen backsplashes and floors

Backsplash layouts are centered on the range, sink and windows so cuts land where they will not be noticed. Kitchen floors are checked for flatness before large-format tile goes down.

Fireplace surrounds

Many split-level family rooms have a brick fireplace wall. Refacing the surround with tile or stone updates the room without rebuilding the fireplace.

Tile on older subfloors

Tile needs a stiff, flat floor to avoid cracked tile and grout. In older homes we check floor deflection and add reinforcement or underlayment where needed.

Local context

Tile in Palmer Township homes

In mid-century homes, original floors and walls are rarely flat, and older tile may be set in a thick mortar bed. Replacing it well means correcting the floor and wall structure first. Newer homes are easier to tile but often have builder-grade shower waterproofing, which we replace with a full membrane system. We test older flooring and adhesive before removal when needed.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can new tile go over existing tile?

Occasionally, if the existing floor is flat, sound and well bonded. In most cases we remove it, since tiling over adds height at doorways and hides problems underneath.

How long does a tiled shower take?

Tile work for a shower usually takes several days to two weeks depending on size, pattern and niches, plus time for the waterproofing and grout to cure.

Is large-format tile a good choice for an older home?

Yes, as long as the floor is flat and stiff enough. We check both before installation and correct the floor where needed.
