Our scope

What our tile installation services include in Williams Township

These are the tile projects we install most often in Williams Township.

Stone and tile entries

Entries take mud and wear from rural properties. We use durable stone or porcelain over a properly prepared floor.

Hearths and fireplace surrounds

Many farmhouses have original fireplaces. We tile or reface surrounds and hearths in materials that suit the age of the house.

Tile that stands up to hard water

Honed and matte finishes and sealed stone show less scale than glossy tile, and coated glass keeps shower enclosures clearer.

Reinforcing old wood floors

We add blocking, new underlayment and an uncoupling membrane so tile can go down on floors that have moved over time.

Hard well water also matters: it leaves scale on glass and glossy tile, so we choose materials with that in mind.

Our approach

Why Williams Township homeowners choose Atlas

We treat the substrate as the most important part of any tile job. A beautiful tile set on a weak floor will crack, so we fix the floor first.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can tile go on an old farmhouse floor?

Usually, once the floor is reinforced and flattened. We check how much the floor flexes and add blocking or underlayment where needed before any tile goes down.

Which shower tile works best with hard well water?

Matte or honed tile and lighter grout show mineral spots less than glossy surfaces. A coated glass enclosure and a regular squeegee routine help even more.
