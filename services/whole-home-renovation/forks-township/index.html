Local context

Whole home renovations in Forks Township homes

Most homes in Forks Township were built in the 1980s, 1990s and 2000s. These are well-built houses in good structural condition, but a significant number were finished with builder-grade materials that simply have not aged well. A whole home renovation here is often less about structural surprises and more about rethinking a layout that no longer fits the way the family actually lives, outdated kitchens, undersized bathrooms, underutilized basements and living areas that feel fragmented rather than connected.

A smaller subset of older farmhouses on the township's remaining agricultural parcels may have conditions more similar to Easton's older housing stock, including older plumbing, electrical and foundation systems. We are EPA Lead Safe certified for any home built before 1978. Regardless of the home's age, we assess every site on its own conditions before any design work begins.

Our scope

What our whole home renovation services include in Forks Township

Whole home renovations in Forks Township usually include several of these.

One plan across the whole house

Updating a 1990s home room by room over years often leaves mismatched flooring, trim and lighting. A whole home plan carries the same materials and details through every room.

First-floor primary suites

Converting a den or office into a primary suite with its own bathroom lets homeowners stay in the house longer without relying on the stairs. We confirm plumbing access before design begins.

Two-story foyers and great rooms

Tall spaces need lighting, railings and millwork scaled to their height. We plan these together so the room feels finished rather than empty.

Restoring older farmhouses

Farmhouse renovations start with structure and systems, then focus on keeping what makes the house worth saving, such as wide plank floors, deep window sills and original stairs.

Lighting and electrical throughout

Many homes here have a single overhead fixture per room. Layered lighting with recessed, accent and decorative fixtures changes how every room feels, and a whole home project is the right time to wire for it.
