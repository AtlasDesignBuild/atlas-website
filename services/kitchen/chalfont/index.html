Local knowledge

Remodeling kitchens in Chalfont and New Britain Township

Chalfont Borough grew around its railroad station after the mid-1800s and was incorporated in 1902. Kitchens in the borough's older homes are typically small, set at the back of the house and often updated piecemeal over the decades, so a remodel usually starts with rethinking the whole room rather than replacing what is there.

New Britain Township, which surrounds much of the borough, is mostly colonials, split levels and larger single family homes built in the second half of the 20th century. The typical kitchen there is separated from the family room by a wall or a half wall, with a breakfast area that takes up space an island could use better.

A Chalfont mailing address can mean Chalfont Borough, New Britain Township or the separate New Britain Borough, each with its own permit process. We also confirm early whether a home runs on natural gas, propane or all-electric service, since that shapes range and appliance choices before the design is set.

Questions

Kitchen remodeling in Chalfont: common questions

Is my home in Chalfont Borough or New Britain Township?

A Chalfont mailing address covers Chalfont Borough, much of New Britain Township and parts of neighboring municipalities. Each issues its own permits, so we confirm the correct one for your property before filing.

Can we switch from an electric range to gas?

Often, yes. If natural gas is available at the street, a new line can usually be run to the kitchen. Where it is not, propane is an option. We look at fuel source and venting early because it affects the range, the hood and the budget.

Should we give up our breakfast nook for an island?

In many New Britain Township colonials, yes. An island with seating usually serves the same purpose while adding prep space and storage. If you want to keep a dedicated table, we can design a built-in banquette that takes less floor space.
