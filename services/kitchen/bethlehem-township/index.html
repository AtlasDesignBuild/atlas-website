Our scope

What our kitchen remodeling services include in Bethlehem Township

These are the kitchen projects we plan most often in Bethlehem Township.

Opening split-level and bi-level kitchens

Kitchens on the upper level of a bi-level or split often sit behind a wall from the living room. Opening it brings in light from the front of the house and connects the main living space.

Making the most of townhome kitchens

Townhome kitchens are often narrow. Full-height cabinetry, a compact island or peninsula and better lighting make them feel larger.

Finishing builder-grade kitchens in newer homes

Newer kitchens usually have the right layout. We replace cabinetry, counters and lighting so the room matches the rest of the house.

Appliance and electrical upgrades

Moving to induction, a larger range or a second oven often needs new circuits. We plan the electrical work before the appliance list is final.

Hoods and metal details made in house

We fabricate range hoods, glass inserts and metal details in house, sized and finished for each kitchen.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you remodel kitchens in townhomes?

Yes. Interior kitchen work in a townhome usually needs only a building permit, but if your community has an HOA, check whether it reviews interior work or contractor access before we schedule.

Is it worth opening the kitchen wall in a bi-level?

In most bi-levels, yes. The wall between the kitchen and living room often carries some load, so we confirm the structure and size a beam if needed. The result is a brighter, more connected main floor.
