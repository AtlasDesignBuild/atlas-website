Local knowledge

Remodeling kitchens in Langhorne, Langhorne Manor and Middletown Township

Most homes with a Langhorne mailing address are in Middletown Township, which grew more than fourfold in the 1950s as Levittown and neighborhoods such as Langhorne Terrace were built. Kitchens in these postwar homes are compact by design and often closed off from the living room, which makes them some of the best candidates in Bucks County for a layout that opens the room up.

Many of these homes sit on concrete slabs, some with radiant heating pipes in the slab. That matters in a kitchen, because moving a sink to an island or relocating a dishwasher means routing a new drain through the slab. We locate existing lines before any concrete is cut and design the layout around what we find.

Langhorne Borough (the historic village) and Langhorne Manor are separate small boroughs with their own permit processes. In Langhorne Borough's historic district, exterior changes such as a new window or door go through the borough's Historic Architectural Review Board, while interior kitchen work follows the standard building permit.

Questions

Kitchen remodeling in Langhorne: common questions

Can we put a sink in the island if our house is on a slab?

Usually, yes, but it requires cutting a trench in the slab for the new drain. In homes with radiant heat in the slab, we locate the heating lines first and route around them. We confirm feasibility at the first visit before the design depends on it.

Is my Langhorne home in the borough or Middletown Township?

Most homes with a Langhorne mailing address are in Middletown Township. Langhorne Borough and Langhorne Manor are small, separate boroughs. Each has its own permit process, and we confirm which applies before filing.

Is opening the kitchen to the living room worth it in a smaller home?

In most postwar homes it is the single change that makes the biggest difference. It brings in light, connects the kitchen to where the family spends time and often allows a more efficient cabinet layout than the original closed room.
