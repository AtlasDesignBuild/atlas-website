Much of Williams Township is rural, with farmhouses that have been added onto over generations and custom homes built on larger lots from the 1980s onward. Farmhouse kitchens often sit in a later addition, with thick original walls on one side and newer framing on the other. Our scope

What our kitchen remodeling services include in Williams Township

These are the kitchen projects we plan most often in Williams Township.

Farmhouse kitchens across old and new walls

Where an addition meets the original house, floors and ceilings rarely line up. We level, reframe and plan cabinetry so the kitchen reads as one room.

Planning for well water

Hard or iron-rich water stains fixtures and shortens appliance life. We leave room for a filtration or softening system and choose finishes that hold up to it.

Islands and work zones for large kitchens

Larger custom kitchens need clear zones for cooking, cleanup and gathering. We size islands and walkways so a big room works as well as it looks.

Pantries and scullery storage

On rural properties, bulk storage matters. Walk-in pantries, scullery sinks and appliance garages keep the main kitchen clear.

Hoods and metal details made in house

Range hoods, glass inserts and metal details are fabricated in house, so each piece suits the kitchen and the age of the home.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does well water affect a kitchen remodel?

It can. Hard or iron-rich water can stain sinks and faucets and shorten the life of dishwashers and refrigerators with water lines. We test your water early and plan space for any treatment system you need.

Can you open up a farmhouse kitchen?

Often, yes, but farmhouse walls can be thick, structural and full of surprises. We open walls only after confirming what they carry, and we plan the new layout around beams and windows worth keeping.
