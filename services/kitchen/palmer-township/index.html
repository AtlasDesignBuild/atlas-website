Local context

Kitchens in Palmer Township homes

Much of Palmer Township's housing falls into two groups. Ranches, split-levels and colonials from the 1950s through the 1970s often have closed kitchens sized for a different way of living, with walls that can be opened to connect the kitchen to dining and family rooms. Homes built from the 1990s onward usually have a workable layout already, so those projects focus on cabinetry, counters, appliances and lighting.

In mid-century homes we test older flooring and adhesive before removal when needed and follow lead-safe practices in homes built before 1978. We are EPA Lead Safe certified.

Our scope

What our kitchen remodeling services include in Palmer Township

Most Palmer Township kitchen projects follow one of a few patterns, depending on when the house was built. These are the ones we plan for most often.

Opening a mid-century closed kitchen

Kitchens in 1950s and 1960s ranches were usually walled off from the dining room. Removing or widening that wall changes how the whole first floor works. We confirm whether the wall is load bearing, size a beam if it is and reroute any plumbing, ductwork or wiring inside it.

Reworking split-level kitchens

Split-level kitchens often sit between the living room and the stairs to the lower level, with limited wall space and a short run of counter. We look at stair openings, window placement and the adjoining dining area to find room for an island or a longer working wall.

Upgrading 1990s and 2000s kitchens

Newer kitchens in Palmer often have a workable layout with oak or thermofoil cabinets, laminate or early granite counters and dated lighting. These projects usually keep the plumbing where it is and put the budget into custom cabinetry, stone, appliances and a proper lighting plan.

Electrical capacity for modern appliances

Mid-century homes were wired for far less than a modern kitchen draws. Induction ranges, double ovens and under-cabinet lighting usually need new dedicated circuits and sometimes a service upgrade, which we plan before cabinets are ordered.

Range hoods and metalwork made in house

We fabricate custom metal range hoods and glass cabinet inserts ourselves, so each piece is sized to the kitchen and finished to match the hardware and fixtures.

Drawings, permits and inspections

We prepare the drawings, apply for the permits Palmer Township requires and schedule every inspection, so the paperwork moves alongside construction.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you remove the wall between the kitchen and dining room in a ranch home?

Usually, yes. Many of these walls carry part of the ceiling or roof load, so we confirm the structure first and, where needed, replace the wall with a properly sized beam. The scope also includes permit drawings, patching the floor where the wall stood and rerouting anything that ran through it.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A refresh that keeps the layout can be finished in a few weeks. A full remodel with layout or structural changes takes several months from design through construction, depending on permits and how much electrical work is involved.

Can we keep using the kitchen during the remodel?

Not during demolition and installation. We can set up a temporary kitchen area elsewhere in the house and plan the schedule so the time without a working kitchen is as short as possible.
