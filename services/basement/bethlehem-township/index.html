Basements in Bethlehem Township homes

Split-levels and bi-levels have partly above-grade lower levels, many already finished decades ago with paneling and older carpet. Colonials and larger homes from the 1990s onward usually have full poured basements with sump pumps, ready to be finished from scratch. Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a permit to update a split-level lower level?

If the work involves new framing, electrical or plumbing, yes. Cosmetic updates such as flooring and paint usually do not. We confirm the requirements for your project with the township.

Should we worry about the sump pump in a finished basement?

It should stay accessible and, ideally, have a battery backup. We frame an access panel or closet around it so it can be serviced without damaging finished walls.
