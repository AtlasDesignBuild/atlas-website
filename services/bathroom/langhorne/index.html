Local knowledge

Remodeling bathrooms in Langhorne, Langhorne Manor and Middletown Township

A Langhorne mailing address covers three different places. Langhorne Borough is the historic village, listed as a historic district, with homes dating back to the 18th and 19th centuries. Langhorne Manor is a small, separate borough. Most Langhorne addresses, though, sit in Middletown Township.

Middletown grew more than fourfold in the 1950s as William Levitt built Levittown across four municipalities, Middletown among them, and neighborhoods such as Langhorne Terrace went up at the same time. Many homes from that period were built with a single full bathroom, and adding a second bathroom or a powder room is one of the most valuable projects in the area.

Many original Levittown homes sit on concrete slabs, some with radiant heating pipes in the slab, so relocating a drain or adding a bathroom takes careful planning before any concrete is cut. In Langhorne Borough, exterior changes within the historic district go through the borough's Historic Architectural Review Board, while interior work follows the standard permit process.

Questions

Bathroom remodeling in Langhorne: common questions

Is my Langhorne home in the borough or Middletown Township?

Most homes with a Langhorne mailing address are in Middletown Township. Langhorne Borough and Langhorne Manor are small, separate boroughs. Each has its own permit process, and we confirm which applies to your property before filing.

Can we add a second bathroom to a Levittown-era home?

In most cases, yes. Common sources of space include a hall closet, part of a bedroom or a reworked laundry area. Because many of these homes sit on slabs, we locate existing plumbing and any in-slab heating first and design the drain route around it.

Does the Langhorne historic district affect a bathroom remodel?

The borough's Historic Architectural Review Board reviews exterior changes within the historic district. Interior bathroom work goes through the standard building permit. If your project changes a window or adds an exterior vent, we include that review in the schedule.
