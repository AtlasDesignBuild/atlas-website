Local context

Bathrooms in Bethlehem Township homes

Split-levels and bi-levels from the 1960s and 1970s often have one full bath upstairs and a half bath on the lower level, with original tile and fixtures. Newer colonials and larger homes from the 1990s onward have bigger primary baths, often with corner tubs and prefabricated showers. Townhomes add their own constraints, with shared walls and tighter floor plans.

Our scope

What our bathroom remodeling services include in Bethlehem Township

Bathroom projects in Bethlehem Township usually start with one of these.

Turning a lower-level half bath into a full bath

Many split-levels have a half bath on the lower level. Adding a shower gives the house a second full bath without an addition.

Updating original 1960s and 1970s bathrooms

We replace dated tile and fixtures, update supply lines and add properly vented exhaust fans.

Replacing corner tubs in newer primary baths

A large walk-in shower or a freestanding tub uses the space a corner tub takes up far better.

Quiet, well-sealed bathrooms in townhomes

Insulation in shared walls, careful drain routing and properly sealed showers protect your home and your neighbor's.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can we add a shower to our lower-level half bath?

Often, yes. It depends on where the drain lines run and whether the floor is on a slab. We check both during the first walkthrough.

Do you remodel bathrooms in townhomes?

Yes. We pay extra attention to shared walls, sound and waterproofing, since a leak or noise can affect the unit next door.
