Local knowledge

Remodeling bathrooms in Warrington Township

Warrington's roots go back to the 1730s, but most of its homes are much newer. The township had fewer than 5,000 residents in 1960 and nearly tripled by 1990 as subdivisions filled in across central Bucks County. It has kept growing since, and today most Warrington homes date from the 1960s onward.

That growth came with builder bathroom packages repeated across entire neighborhoods: the same tile, the same vanity profiles, the same fixtures. Many Warrington homes have two and a half bathrooms from the same era, all dated at once. The bathrooms work, but they were never designed.

Because several bathrooms often need attention, we frequently plan Warrington projects in phases, starting with the primary bathroom and following with the hall and guest baths on a schedule that suits the household. Warrington Township handles permitting directly, and we factor its review time into every schedule.

Questions

Bathroom remodeling in Warrington: common questions

Can we remodel more than one bathroom at a time?

Yes, although we usually recommend phasing so the household always has a working bathroom. Designing every bathroom at once and building in stages keeps the finishes consistent and often shortens the overall timeline.

Do we need to move out during a bathroom remodel in Warrington?

Almost never. We contain dust at the work area, protect the path through the house and schedule noisy work during the day. Most Warrington homes have at least one other bathroom available throughout the project.

Who issues bathroom remodeling permits in Warrington?

Warrington Township issues building permits for homes within the township. Any remodel that changes plumbing, electrical or the layout needs one. We prepare the application, pull the permit and coordinate each inspection.
