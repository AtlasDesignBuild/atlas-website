Local knowledge

Remodeling bathrooms in Chalfont and New Britain Township

Chalfont Borough was incorporated in 1902 and grew around its railroad station, which still serves the town today. The borough sits between the north and main branches of the Neshaminy Creek, and its historic district holds a mix of 19th and early 20th century homes with compact bathrooms, older plumbing and plaster walls.

Much of the surrounding area is New Britain Township, where most homes are colonials, split levels and larger single family homes built in the second half of the 20th century. Bathrooms there typically have enough room but dated, builder-grade finishes and layouts that were never planned around storage or light.

A Chalfont mailing address can mean Chalfont Borough, New Britain Township or the separate New Britain Borough, each with its own permit process. For homes near the creek branches, we plan lower level bathrooms with extra attention to waterproofing, drainage and ventilation.

Questions

Bathroom remodeling in Chalfont: common questions

Is my home in Chalfont Borough or New Britain Township?

A Chalfont mailing address covers Chalfont Borough, much of New Britain Township and parts of neighboring municipalities. Each issues its own permits, so we confirm the correct one for your property before filing.

Can we add a bathroom in the basement?

Usually, yes. If the new fixtures sit below the main sewer line, an ejector pump carries waste up to it. For homes close to the Neshaminy Creek branches, we also look closely at waterproofing and drainage before committing to a basement bathroom.

How long does a bathroom remodel take in Chalfont?

Most projects run four to eight weeks of active construction once design is final and the permit is issued. Older borough homes can take a little longer if framing or plumbing needs attention. We share the full schedule in writing before work begins.
