What our interior millwork services include in Bethlehem Township

Millwork projects in Bethlehem Township usually start with one of these.

Stair and railing updates in split-levels

Local context

Millwork in Bethlehem Township homes

Split-levels and bi-levels usually have slim trim, open stair rails and few built-ins. Colonials from the 1990s and 2000s have larger rooms with standard builder profiles. Both benefit from fuller casings, paneling and cabinetry designed for the room.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can you update the railings on a split-level staircase?

Yes. We replace railings, balusters and newel posts and can add paneled half walls, all built to current code for height and spacing.

Which room should we start with?

Usually the room you see most often, such as the entry, stair hall or family room. Starting there sets the profiles and finishes for the rest of the house.
