Interior millwork in Forks Township, PA

Forks Township is one of the fastest growing communities in the Lehigh Valley — a mix of established single-family neighborhoods, newer construction from the 1980s through the 2000s and a handful of remaining farmhouses from its earlier history as agricultural land. Most homes here were built with minimal architectural trim: flat door casings, simple baseboards, smooth ceilings and few built-ins. Custom interior millwork changes that in a lasting way, adding proportion, depth and character to rooms that currently have none. Local context

Millwork in Forks Township homes

Most homes in Forks Township were built in the 1980s, 1990s and 2000s with the trim profile that was standard for that era: flat or colonial-style door casings, simple baseboards, smooth drywall ceilings and no built-ins to speak of. These homes are well-built and in good structural condition, but the interior finishes leave room for significant improvement. Adding millwork to a 1990s colonial — wainscoting in a dining room, a coffered ceiling in a living room, custom built-ins flanking a fireplace — transforms how the home reads and how it holds value over time.

Because these homes have consistent framing and standard ceiling heights, millwork installation here is often more straightforward than in older homes. There are fewer hidden variables behind the walls and less variability in the substrate. We still assess every space before design work begins, but the starting point is typically clean.
