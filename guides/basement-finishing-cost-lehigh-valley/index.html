How much does it cost to finish a basement in the Lehigh Valley?

By Rachel Foy · Published October 20, 2026

Finishing a basement in the Lehigh Valley typically runs $35,000 to $180,000. A simple open living space sits at the low end. A full lower level with a bathroom, wet bar and built-ins sits at the high end. The condition of the basement you start with matters as much as the finishes you choose, and that varies widely between an Easton rowhome and a newer home in Forks or Palmer Township.

Why Lehigh Valley basements vary in cost

Three things set the budget before any design decisions are made: moisture, ceiling height and plumbing. A dry basement with eight-foot ceilings and a bathroom rough-in already in the slab is a very different starting point than a damp stone-walled cellar with low beams and ductwork. Once those are understood, the remaining cost follows the number of rooms and the level of finish.

What your basement starts with

Newer homes in Forks Township, Palmer Township and other developments built from the 1980s onward usually have poured concrete walls, reasonable ceiling heights and dry conditions. Some already have a bathroom rough-in in the slab, which lowers the cost of adding a bathroom considerably.

Older homes in Easton's College Hill, West Ward and South Side neighborhoods often have stone or block foundations, lower ceilings and more moisture. These basements can be finished well, but they need more preparation before framing begins.

What older Lehigh Valley homes add to the scope

In older basements we plan for:

  • Moisture management, which can range from improved drainage and a dehumidifier to interior waterproofing
  • Stone or rubble foundation walls that need sealing and a properly detailed wall system
  • Low ceilings, beams and ductwork that limit layout or require soffits
  • Egress windows for any space used as a bedroom, which add excavation and cutting through the foundation
  • Electrical service that may need an upgrade to support new circuits

We address moisture first on every project. Finishing over an unresolved water issue is the most expensive mistake in basement work.

How this compares to Bucks County

Basement finishing costs in the Lehigh Valley are comparable to Bucks County. The same materials, trades and building codes apply. The difference between two projects almost always comes from the basement's condition and the scope, not the location. Frequently asked questions

Do you finish basements outside Easton and Forks Township?

Yes. We also work in Palmer Township, Williams Township and other nearby Lehigh Valley towns. Call us to confirm your address is within our service area.

How long does it take to finish a basement?

Active construction usually takes six to ten weeks after design and permits are complete. Most projects take three to five months from the first conversation to the final walkthrough. Bathrooms, wet bars and moisture work extend both timelines.

Can you finish a basement with a stone foundation?

Yes. Stone foundations are common in older Easton homes. They need careful moisture management and a wall system designed to let the foundation breathe, which we build into the scope from the start.

Do I need a permit to finish a basement in the Lehigh Valley?

Yes. Finishing a basement requires a building permit, and adding a bedroom requires an egress window that meets code. Requirements vary between the City of Easton and the surrounding townships, and we handle permitting on every project.

Is finishing a basement worth it?

A finished basement adds living space for far less per square foot than an addition, because the foundation, structure and roof already exist. The best results come from solving moisture and layout first and choosing finishes second.
