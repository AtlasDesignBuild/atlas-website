How much does a kitchen remodel cost in the Lehigh Valley?

By Rachel Foy · Kitchen remodeling · Published September 26, 2026

Homeowners in Easton, Forks Township and the surrounding Lehigh Valley are asking the same question homeowners in Bucks County ask us every day, what does a kitchen remodel actually cost. The honest answer depends on scope, finish level and the condition of what is already there, but the ranges below give a real starting point based on the kitchens we build.

We work across Bucks County, Montgomery County and the Lehigh Valley, and we bring the same design and construction standard to a kitchen in Easton that we bring to one in Doylestown. This guide walks through what drives kitchen remodel cost in the Lehigh Valley specifically, including the older housing stock common throughout Easton and Forks Township.

Why Lehigh Valley kitchens vary in cost

A kitchen remodel in Easton or Forks Township can range from a cosmetic refresh to a full gut renovation, and cost follows scope more than location. What does shift by region is the age and construction of the housing stock. Much of the Lehigh Valley, like much of Bucks County, is built around older stone and brick homes with original layouts that were not designed around a modern kitchen, which affects both scope and cost.

Cosmetic refresh versus full remodel

A cosmetic refresh keeps the existing layout and plumbing and updates finishes, cabinet fronts, counters, backsplash, paint. A full remodel changes the layout, moves plumbing or electrical, and often opens the kitchen to an adjacent room. The first keeps cost more contained. The second costs more but is often the only way to fix a layout that no longer works for how a home is actually used.

What older Lehigh Valley homes add to the scope

Homes throughout Easton and Forks Township frequently have original plaster walls, older electrical service, and layouts built before open concept living became the norm. Opening a wall in one of these homes sometimes reveals a load-bearing condition that needs a structural fix, and older electrical panels sometimes need an upgrade to support a modern kitchen's appliance load. We plan for this possibility rather than treating it as a surprise once a project is underway.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work on kitchens outside Easton and Forks Township in the Lehigh Valley?

Absolutely. We also work in Palmer Township, Williams Township and other surrounding Lehigh Valley towns. Give us a call at (484) 541-9818 to check whether your address falls within our service area.

Is a kitchen remodel more expensive in an older Easton home than new construction?

It can be, mainly because of what is often found once walls or ceilings are opened, outdated electrical, plaster repair, or a layout that needs structural changes to open up. We account for this in scoping rather than treating it as an unplanned add-on.

How long does a typical kitchen remodel take?

A cosmetic refresh can often be completed in a few weeks. A full remodel with layout changes typically takes several months, depending on permitting and the scope of structural or electrical work involved.

Do I need a permit for a kitchen remodel in the Lehigh Valley?

Most remodels involving plumbing, electrical or structural changes require a permit, and requirements vary by township. We handle permitting as part of the project rather than leaving it to the homeowner to navigate.

What is the biggest cost driver in a kitchen remodel?

Cabinetry is typically the largest single line item, followed by counters and appliances. Layout changes that involve moving plumbing or opening walls add cost beyond finish selections alone.

Can I remodel my kitchen in phases to spread out the cost?

Some projects can be phased, cosmetic updates first, layout changes later, though phasing works better when it is planned from the start rather than decided partway through.
