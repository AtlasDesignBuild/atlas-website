How we work

The Atlas process

Every Atlas project follows the same six steps. You know what is happening, when it is happening and what comes next, from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.

The six steps

From first conversation
to final walkthrough

01

Discovery call

A 20 to 30 minute conversation about your home, your goals and your timeline. We talk openly about investment so you know early whether we are the right fit. Complimentary.

02

Site consultation

We visit your home to see the space, take initial measurements and talk through your ideas and inspiration. Within two business days you receive a written budget range for your project. Complimentary.

03

Design

This is where your project takes shape. We take full measurements and develop your layout, drawings and selections, refining them with you over two rounds of revisions. The design phase ends with an exact fixed price for your project. Your design fee is credited toward construction.

04

Proposal

We present your complete proposal: scope, finishes, investment and timeline in one place. Your start date is set once the agreement is signed.

05

Construction

We protect your home, keep the site organized and send you an update every Friday covering what was completed, what comes next and any decisions we need from you. You walk through each milestone with us before it is invoiced.

06

Walkthrough and warranty

We walk every space with you and complete any final details. You receive a closeout package with your warranty, product list and care guide. We check in at 30 days and again before your one year mark.

Questions

Common questions

Why is there a fee for design?

Design is where the real decisions about your home are made. It takes careful measurement, planning and pricing, and that work is what lets us give you an exact price rather than an estimate.

Does every project include a design phase?

No. Smaller projects such as tile installation, or work with no layout changes, go straight from the site consultation to a fixed price proposal.

Is the fee credited toward construction?

Yes. When you sign your construction agreement within 30 days of your proposal presentation, the full design fee is credited.

Will I know the cost before paying for design?

Yes. After the site consultation you receive a written budget range, so you can decide on the design phase with a clear picture of the overall investment.

How long does the design phase take?

Typically two to four weeks for a bathroom, four to six weeks for a kitchen and six to ten weeks for a whole home. Timing depends on scope, how quickly feedback and decisions come back and the availability of our trade partners for pricing. The schedule pauses while we wait on feedback or decisions.

What if my project comes in above my budget?

We watch the budget throughout design and raise it early. We can adjust scope or finish level so the design stays aligned with your investment range.

How do we stay informed during construction?

You receive a written update every Friday, and we reply to messages within one business day. If anything unexpected comes up, we call you the same day.
